Quantitative regulatory genomics

Our world revolves around transcription factors

How do transcription factors read the genome—and reshape its function?

We combine computation and experiment to uncover how protein–DNA interactions regulate genes, influence genome editing, and alter genome stability.

What We Do

We study how transcription factors recognize DNA and how those interactions shape gene regulation, genetic variation, DNA repair, and mutagenesis.

Explore our research ›
Why We Do It

Genome regulation and genome stability are inseparable. Understanding their connection can reveal how mutations arise and how regulatory programs change in disease.

How We Do It

Our lab integrates high-throughput experiments, quantitative biochemistry, genomic analysis, machine learning, and cell-based studies.

Who We Are

We are a collaborative team of experimental and computational scientists united by a shared interest in protein–DNA recognition.
